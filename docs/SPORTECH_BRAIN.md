## 1. WHAT SPORTECH ACTUALLY IS

**Sportech** (brand name — drop "Innovate" on the website) is a **B2B sports technology infrastructure company** that builds a white-label fan engagement super-app and deploys it for football leagues and clubs at zero upfront cost. The clubs brand it as their own. Fans subscribe through it. Sportech takes 50% of all revenue generated. The club takes 50% and brings nothing but access and brand.

**The one-line pitch to clubs:** "We build it, we run it, you put your badge on it, we split the money."

**Core positioning:** The revenue infrastructure layer between sports clubs and their fans — turning passive spectators into multiple streams of digital income for the club. NOT football-only — any sport, any league, any club globally.

**The key distinction from Socios.com:** Socios is a fan token/single-revenue-stream product. Sportech is a full engagement operating system that generates revenue across 8+ streams simultaneously — subscriptions, live streaming, predictions, voting, merchandise, digital cards, NFTs, collectibles — all native inside the club's own app, not a third-party platform. Clubs own the fan relationship. It's the difference between renting a market stall and owning the whole marketplace.

**Analogy for website:** Like TikTok-level live engagement, but it's native to each club's app and every interaction has a monetization layer.

## 2. THE PRODUCT

### The Platform (Fans Interactive Platform)
A white-label mobile super-app that clubs deploy under their own branding ("Whitelabel"). Each club gets a fully customized version. Features are modular — clubs can activate/deactivate from a central admin panel.

### The Signature IP: Player POV Camera ✅ PATENTED HARDWARE
- **Cameras physically mounted on players' jerseys** — live streams from inside the field, from the player's point of view
- **Patent confirmed: covers the camera hardware itself**
- Fans access player camera feeds in real-time through the app
- Different subscription tiers unlock different levels of camera access
- No major global club platform (PSG, Barca, Man United) has this
- This is the single most differentiating feature in the global sports tech market

### Full Feature Set

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Player Camera** | Live jersey-mounted POV streaming during matches |
| **PredictPro** | Match/player prediction engine using platform digital currency (Sharia-compliant, not cash betting) |
| **Interactive Voting** | Fans vote on tactical decisions (substitutions, formations), admin decisions — vote weight scales with subscription tier |
| **Live Rooms (Audio Spaces)** | Private/public audio rooms with live match data for fan discussions |
| **Augmented Reality** | Real-time stats/data overlay on match view; compatible with AR glasses |
| **Live Commentator Feed** | Fans listen to in-stadium commentators through the app |
| **Ticket Purchase & Resale** | Integrated ticketing system |
| **Merchandise Store** | Physical goods with Aramex delivery |

## 3. BUSINESS MODEL ✅ CONFIRMED

### The Deal Structure
**Sportech charges clubs zero upfront. Zero license fee. Zero setup cost.**
Sportech builds, deploys, operates, and maintains the entire platform.
In return: **Sportech takes 50% of all revenue generated. Club keeps 50%.**

This is the entire pitch to clubs: no risk, no cost, pure upside.

### Club's Obligations (what they bring)
- Official exclusive content (news before it hits X/social media)
- Player access: min. 1 community livestream/month, player cameras active in min. 20 matches/season
- Club brand/trademark for white-labeling
- Marketing through official club channels
- Appointment of official platform ambassador

### Sportech's Obligations (what they bring)
- Full platform build, hosting, and operation
- Cybersecurity and data protection
- 24/7 member technical support
- Monthly/quarterly performance reporting
- Continuous feature updates

### The 8+ Revenue Streams (Sportech takes 50% of all of these)

| Revenue Stream | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Subscriptions** | Fan, Star, SuperStar, Partner tiers |
| **Digital Currency (PredictPro tokens)** | Fans buy tokens to make predictions |
| **Sports Predictions** | Prediction outcomes, rewards in platform currency |
| **Voting** | Higher tiers pay for more votes / initiate votes |
| **Ticket Sales** | In-app ticket purchase and peer-to-peer resale |
| **Merchandise Store** | Physical goods (jerseys, accessories) via Aramex |
| **Digital Gift Cards** | Club-branded cards accepted at 150,000+ stores globally |
| **NFT / Collectibles** | Digital trading cards, rare items, seasonal collections |

### Fan Subscription Tiers (SAR pricing from SPL deployments)

| Tier | Price | Key Privileges |
|---|---|---|
